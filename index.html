Complexity guide

1 — Very easy: few mechanics to track. 2 — Easy: a handful of tactical decisions. 3 — Moderate: multiple interacting powers or resources. 4 — Complex: substantial timing or build decisions. 5 — Very complex: extensive choices, reaction timing, unusual systems, or resource tracking.

These are advisory estimates, not official five-point ratings. They're based on baseline class play, not every possible build. Extra options can make a class more complicated without necessarily making it stronger.

Using Dark Alleys & Twisted Paths

You may select supplemental talents, spells, feats, and powers where they are legal for your class and level. The separate Third-party options links in the table go to the SRD's relevant expansion page. These pages can include material from several supplements, so check the individual entry's source label: DATP means Dark Alleys & Twisted Paths, while DPAS means Dark Pacts & Ancient Secrets.

The expansions can give simpler classes more flexibility and interesting choices, especially the Barbarian and Paladin. They don't guarantee parity between builds, however. Check unusual interactions with the GM, particularly multiclassing and effects that grant extra attacks or combine strong defensive features.

Where the table has no separate expansion link, the class's own SRD page is the starting point for its class options. A missing extra link does not imply that the class has no legal third-party support.
